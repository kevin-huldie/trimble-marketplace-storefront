Round edges corners 2 helps you soften hard edges on cabinetry, furniture, and architectural details directly inside SketchUp. Select edges or faces, set a radius, and apply consistent fillets without leaving the modeling environment.

Key features

  • Rounds corners of all selected edges
  • Live preview while setting radius
  • Uniform or variable radius per edge selection
  • Works on both open and closed edge loops
  • Preserves original geometry for easy undo and iteration
